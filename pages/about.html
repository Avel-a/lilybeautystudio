About Lily Beauty's Studio

Lily Beauty's Studio is a salon that was established in 2020 in Port Elizabeth. The salon is owned by a 24-year-old woman originally from Harare, Zimbabwe. She moved to South Africa to pursue better opportunities and grow her business Lily Beauty's Studio offers professional hair care services,including hair wash, treatment, wig installation and styling, and braiding. Over the years, the business grew rapidly because of customer referrals and the quality of its services. The salon is nowlooking to increase its exposure and strengthen its online presence.

Our Mission

To provide top quality, affordable hair care service, while instilling confidence in every client.


Our Vision

To be the number one destination in Port Elizabeth for women's hair care.


Our Target Customers

Lily Beauty's Studio provides services to:

  • Women between the ages of 15-45
  • Young professionals and students
  • Locals
