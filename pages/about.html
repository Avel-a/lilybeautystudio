About Lily Beauty's Studio

Top quality hair care that is affordable, and a place where every client leaves feeling confident.

1

Lily Beauty's Studio is a salon that was established in 2020 in Port Elizabeth, South Africa.

The salon is owned by a 24-year-old woman originally from Harare, Zimbabwe. She moved to South Africa to pursue better opportunities and grow her business.

Over the years, the business has grown through customer referrals and the quality of its services. The salon is now looking to increase its exposure and strengthen its online presence.

2

Lily Beauty's Studio offers professional hair care services designed to help clients look and feel confident.

  • Hair wash
  • Hair treatment
  • Wig installation and styling
  • Professional braiding
3

To provide top-quality, affordable hair care services while instilling confidence in every client.

4

To be the number one destination in Port Elizabeth for women's hair care.

5

Lily Beauty's Studio provides services to:

  • Women between the ages of 15–45
  • Young professionals and students
  • Local women in the community
